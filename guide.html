Travel Tips

Practical guidance to help you Kathmandu trip go smoothly, from
getting around to temple etiquette.

Title

Kathmandu is pleasant most of the year, but conditions shift a lot by season.

  • Oct–Nov: clear skies, best mountain views, peak season
  • Dec–Feb: cool and dry, chilly mornings and evenings
  • Mar–May: warm, hazy at times, spring flowers in bloom
  • Jun–Sep: monsoon rains, lush but slippery trails
