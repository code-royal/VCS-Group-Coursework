Travel Tips

A few practical essentials for a respectful, comfortable, and memorable time exploring Kathmandu.

Best Time to Visit

Kathmandu changes beautifully with the seasons, so plan around the experience you want most.

  • Oct–Nov: clear skies and mountain views
  • Dec–Feb: dry days and cool mornings
  • Mar–May: warm weather and spring blooms
  • Jun–Sep: monsoon rain and lush landscapes

Getting Around

The city is lively, compact in places, and best explored with a little patience and flexibility.

  • Walk short distances through heritage areas
  • Use ride-sharing apps for easy trips
  • Agree on taxi fares before starting
  • Allow extra time during peak traffic

Temple Etiquette

Kathmandu’s temples are active places of worship. A respectful visit makes the experience better for everyone.

  • Remove shoes where signs ask you to
  • Dress modestly at religious sites
  • Walk clockwise around stupas
  • Ask before photographing rituals

Food & Water

From momo stalls to traditional Newari meals, Kathmandu offers plenty to try—just make thoughtful choices.

  • Choose busy, well-reviewed food spots
  • Drink sealed or filtered water
  • Try momo, dal bhat, and chatamari
  • Carry hand sanitiser for street food stops

Money & Shopping

Cash is still useful in local markets, small cafés, and many taxi rides throughout the city.

  • Keep small Nepali rupee notes handy
  • Cards work at many hotels and restaurants
  • Bargain politely in markets
  • Buy handmade crafts from trusted shops

Stay Prepared

Kathmandu rewards curious travelers who are prepared for changing weather, busy streets, and a little dust.

  • Bring a light jacket for cool evenings
  • Carry sunscreen and a reusable bottle
  • Pack a mask for dusty roads
  • Save offline maps before heading out
