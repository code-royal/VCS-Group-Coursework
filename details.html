Garden of Dreams

Overview

Perched on a forested hill west of the old city, Swayambhunath is one of the oldest and most sacred Buddhist sites in the world. The whitewashed stupa is watched over by the painted eyes of the Buddha on all four sides, and prayer wheels line the climb to the top. Resident monkeys give the site its popular nickname, and the summit offers one of the best panoramic views of the Kathmandu Valley, especially at sunrise or sunset.
