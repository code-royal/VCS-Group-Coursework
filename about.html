Our Mission

Explore Kathmandu started as a simple idea: the Kathmandu Valley has an extraordinary density of temples, viewpoints, markets, and hidden corners, but that richness can be overwhelming to plan around. We built this guide to make it easy to browse what’s worth seeing, save the places that catch your eye, and turn that shortlist into a real day-by-day plan without needing an account, an app download, or a data connection once the page has loaded.

A Valley of Many Cultures

Kathmandu sits at a crossroads of Hindu, Buddhist, and Newari traditions that have shaped the valley for over a thousand years. Pagoda-roofed temples share courtyards with monasteries, ancient trade routes are still lined with metalworkers and potters, and festivals fill the streets throughout the year. Whether you have three days or three weeks, there’s always another layer to discover.
